# Recruit Outreach — Templates

For the **10 r/minipainting recruits** step in the launch path. Goal: 7 of 10 say "I'd pay for this." Honest, no marketing-speak, no begging.

Cross-brand paint library (~6k paints, 30+ brands) with colour matching, recipe builder, project tracker that handles armies → units → individual models. It's like Citadel's app but you build your own recipes + works for any brand.
>
> Looking for ~10 people to kick the tires for an hour and tell me what's broken or missing. Free forever for the basic tier (sign-up is username + password, no email, no credit card).
>
> Live at https://miniaturemanager.vercel.app. If you've got an hour to poke around and share thoughts, I'd love your eyes on it.

## Reddit post — long version (for r/minipainting)

**Title:** Built a paint-planning app — looking for 10 hobbyists to try it and tell me what's missing

> Painter here. Built a web app over the past few months because every existing option felt half-finished for the way I actually work — the Citadel app is locked to GW recipes, BrushForge is great for cross-brand swaps but doesn't track projects, the paint-stash trackers don't handle recipes. So I built one that does all three.
>
> **What it does**
> - Cross-brand paint library — ~6k paints from 30+ brands, search and filter by colour / type / brand
> - Recipe builder — define zones (Carapace, Pauldron trim, etc.) and the paint sequence for each. Reuse the same recipe across a whole army.
> - Project tracker — armies → units → individual models, each with a Built / Primed / Painting / Done counter that bubbles up
> - Cross-brand colour matching — point at a Citadel paint, find the closest match in Vallejo / AK / The Army Painter / Scale75 / etc.
> - Wishlist with auto-stock tracking from Element Games / Wayland / Goblin Gaming
>
> **What I want from you**
> - Spend ~30 mins poking around with a real project of yours
> - Tell me what felt obvious and what felt weird
> - Tell me what's missing that would make you actually use this instead of your current setup
>
> **What you get**
> - Free forever for the basic tier (1 project, 1 recipe, 3 wishlist items — enough to evaluate)
> - First 10 testers: Founder Edition free if you want to upgrade later (lifetime Pro + your name on the about page)
> - Direct line to me to ask for features or report bugs
>
> Sign-up takes 20 seconds — username + password, no email, no credit card. Live at https://miniaturemanager.vercel.app. Drop a comment or DM if you're game.

## Follow-up DM after they sign up

> Thanks for signing up. A few things that'll help me get the most signal from your test run:
>
> 1. Try to build a **real** project — an actual army or unit you're planning, not a throwaway. The flow gets weirder the more you make it real.
> 2. If you stumble on something, screenshot it and tell me what you EXPECTED to happen. The "expected" half is where the gold is.
> 3. The honest answer to "would you pay $4/month or $29 once for this" is what I'm trying to figure out. Tell me even if it's no.
>
> No need for a formal report — bullet points, Discord messages, voice notes, whatever's fastest for you. Cheers.

## What NOT to say

- Don't apologise for the app being early. Lead with what it does, not what it doesn't.
- Don't promise features that aren't shipped ("we're adding X next week"). Promise nothing.
- Don't pitch the price unless they ask. The Reddit post mentions $4 / $29; that's enough.
- Don't follow up more than twice. If they signed up and went quiet, they went quiet.
